Common questions

How do I play?
Tap to play, then pull back on the screen and let go to launch from the slingshot. While you slide, drag left and right to steer. Tap Jump and Rocket once you've unlocked them in Skills.
I bought diamonds or the Starter Pack but didn't get them.
Close the game completely and open it again; unfinished purchases are picked up automatically when the game starts. For the Starter Pack, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. If it's still missing, email us. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com (or by Google Play on Android).
Will I lose my progress if I change phones?
Progress is saved on your device. On iPhone it moves to a new phone when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
How do the leaderboards work?
Sign in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). Every finished run posts its distance, and your best counts on the All-time leaderboard and on This Week, which resets every 7 days. Tap the 🏆 button in the game to see them.
Can I turn off the music or sounds?
Yes. Open Settings in the game to adjust music and sound volume. You can also change the camera zoom and height there.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. You can play offline. Purchases and leaderboards need a connection when you use them.
