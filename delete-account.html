Delete your account and data

Sling & Slide by Matthew Lees

Playing Sling & Slide doesn't need an account. The only account we keep is your Global Rankings entry, created when you play signed in to Google Play Games (Android), Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google on slingandslide.com. You can delete it at any time.

Delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Sling & Slide and tap Settings (the gear on the Garage screen).
  2. In Global Rankings, tap Remove me from Global Rankings, then tap it again to confirm.

On the website (slingandslide.com/play): Settings → Delete account.

Your rankings account is deleted from our server straight away, and the game won't join Global Rankings again unless you choose Join Global Rankings.

Or ask us by email

Email slingandslide@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account, and tell us the name you appear as in Global Rankings. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Google Play Games, Game Center or Google account ID, and the random player ID shown in rankings
  • Your rankings name and your rankings entry (best distances, scores and heights, character and level)
  • Your sign-in tokens, and (for website accounts) your nickname and the game progress saved to your account

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept on our server after deletion. There's no extra retention period.
  • Your game progress stored on your phone stays there (delete the game to remove it).
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play, Apple or Stripe under their own policies, not by us.

See the privacy policy for everything the game stores.
