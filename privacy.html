Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Sling & Slide doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. Purchases and leaderboards are handled by Apple (or Google on Android) under their own privacy policies.

This policy explains how the Sling & Slide game ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the game on iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

Information we collect

None. We don't run servers that receive data from the game, and the game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, diamonds, upgrades, unlocked items, records and settings) is saved only on your device, inside the game. It is included in your device's normal backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have them turned on. We can't see it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (or by Google Play on Android). We never receive your name, payment details or billing address. The game receives a transaction ID from Apple or Google, which it keeps on your device so a purchase can't be lost or credited twice. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Game Center leaderboards

On iPhone and iPad you can sign in to Apple's Game Center. If you do, the game sends your run distances to Game Center leaderboards, and other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores there. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy. You can sign out or change your Game Center visibility at any time in your device's Settings → Game Center.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share any data.

Children

Sling & Slide is rated 13+ on the App Store. Because the game collects no personal information, it doesn't collect information from children either. Purchases are protected by Apple's and Google's parental controls, such as Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
