Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short: Sling & Slide doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. Purchases and leaderboards are handled by Apple (or Google on Android) under their own privacy policies.

This policy explains how the Sling & Slide game ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. It applies to the game on iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

Information we collect

The game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The only things we keep are for Global Rankings, and only if you take part: in the app that happens through Game Center (see Global Rankings in the app); on this website, when you sign in (see Website accounts and rankings). Buying something on the website is covered under Website purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, diamonds, upgrades, unlocked items, records and settings) is saved only on your device, inside the game. It is included in your device's normal backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have them turned on. We can't see it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (or by Google Play on Android). We never receive your name, payment details or billing address. The game receives a transaction ID from Apple or Google, which it keeps on your device so a purchase can't be lost or credited twice. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Global Rankings in the app

If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone or iPad, the game joins Global Rankings (the in-game boards for distance, score and height) for you. Game Center gives the app a signed proof of which Game Center player you are, which our server checks with Apple. On our server (run on Cloudflare) we then keep:

  • your Game Center player ID for this game (a code from Apple, not your Apple Account), and a separate random player ID that is shown in rankings instead of it;
  • your Game Center name, which is shown next to your scores;
  • your rankings entry (character, level and best distances, scores and heights);
  • a sign-in token for the app.

Your name and rankings entry are public: anyone playing can see them in Global Rankings, in the app and on our website. We don't use any of this for advertising or tracking. Settings → Remove me from Global Rankings permanently deletes it from our server, and the game won't join again unless you choose Join Global Rankings. If you're not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

Website accounts and rankings

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google while playing at slingandslide.com/play, Google tells us a unique ID for your Google account. We don't store your email address, name or photo. On our server (run on Cloudflare) we keep:

  • that Google account ID, and a separate random player ID that is shown in rankings instead of it;
  • the nickname you choose;
  • your game progress (the same save that is kept in your browser), so you can play on other devices and don't lose it if you clear your browser;
  • your rankings entry (nickname, character, level and best distances, scores and heights);
  • a sign-in token for each browser you're signed in on.

Your nickname and rankings entry are public: anyone playing can see them in Global Rankings. Your progress is private to your account. You can sign out at any time, and Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, saved progress and rankings entry from our server (the progress in your browser stays). Google's sign-in button is provided by Google and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy; it only loads when you open Settings or Global Rankings on the website. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in.

Website purchases

If you buy diamonds or the Starter Pack while playing at slingandslide.com/play, the payment is handled by Stripe on its secure checkout page. Stripe collects your payment details and email address (for your receipt). We never see your full card number. From Stripe we can see the purchase, your email address, the country, brand and last four digits of your card, and any name you enter. We use this only to send receipts, handle refunds and support, and keep the tax records the law requires. Our small shop server (run on Cloudflare) confirms the payment with Stripe and passes the purchase to your game. It also receives a random ID that your browser creates so the purchase goes to the right game. It doesn't store anything else about your purchase. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Game Center leaderboards

On iPhone and iPad you can sign in to Apple's Game Center. If you do, the game also sends your run distances and scores to Game Center's own leaderboards, and other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores there. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy. You can sign out or change your Game Center visibility at any time in your device's Settings → Game Center.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share any data.

Children

Sling & Slide is rated 13+ on the App Store. The game doesn't ask anyone for personal information; the only things we keep are the optional Global Rankings and website account details described above, and players can remove those at any time. Purchases are protected by Apple's and Google's parental controls, such as Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
