Inkshot privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Inkshot is a puzzle game made by Matthew Lees, an independent developer in Australia. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it doesn’t collect any.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, stars, settings and the pages you build in the Workshop are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores (pages cleared, total stars, and your time and ink on the daily page) to Apple’s Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles this under Apple’s privacy policy. We never see your Apple ID or contact details.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

Inkshot has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sign-up. It works offline.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? See the support page.
