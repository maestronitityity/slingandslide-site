Inkshot support

Questions, bugs or ideas for new pages? Email inkshotsupport@proton.me and include your device and the page number if it’s about a puzzle.

Common questions

How do I play? Draw a line on the page with your finger. It becomes a ramp. Press Fire and guide the ball into the hole by the red flag. Less ink earns more stars.

How do I zoom on big Story XL pages? Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to look around. The fit button on the right shows the whole page.

How do I earn coins? Steer the ball through the coins on a page and sink it. Coins only count on a run that finishes in the hole.

I bought coins but didn’t get them. Close and reopen the game; purchases that were interrupted are added when it starts. If they still don’t appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

Will my progress move to a new phone? Progress is saved on the device only, for now.
